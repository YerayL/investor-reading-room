And one thing was, how do you ensure that somebody in rural Kenya, with very little education or no education, can receive an SMS and knows it's money. And this is a simple mom-and-pop shop, and we mapped it to Coca-Cola. That in every little village is a Coca-Cola shop. So in every little place we put M-PESA agent outlets.
